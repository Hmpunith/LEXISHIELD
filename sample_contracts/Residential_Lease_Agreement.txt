RESIDENTIAL LEASE AND OCCUPANCY AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Metro Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Resident ("Tenant").

1. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND DEDUCTIONS
Tenant shall deposit the sum of $3,000 as security. Landlord may retain any portion of the security deposit in Landlord's sole and absolute discretion for administrative re-leasing fees. Landlord shall have up to ninety (90) days following move-out to provide an accounting.

2. ACCESS AND INSPECTION
Landlord and its maintenance agents may enter the leased premises at any time without prior notice for routine inspections, prospective buyer showings, or property evaluation.

3. MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES
Tenant shall be solely responsible for all maintenance, repairs, plumbing fixtures, heating systems, and electrical components within the unit, regardless of fault or pre-existing condition.

4. AUTOMATIC LEASE RENEWAL
This lease automatically renews for consecutive twelve (12) month terms unless Tenant delivers certified written notice of non-renewal exactly ninety (90) calendar days prior to expiration.

5. INDEMNITY AND DAMAGE WAIVER
Tenant waives all claims against Landlord for personal injury, property water damage, roof leaks, or theft occurring on the premises. Tenant agrees to hold Landlord harmless from any guest claims.
